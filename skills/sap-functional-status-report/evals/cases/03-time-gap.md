# 03 — Time that does not close

## Prompt

> #status 14/11
> 9h-11h reunião de refinamento da GAP144
> 11h-12h atualizei a EF com o que ficou decidido
> 14h-16h executei cenários de teste da CR80, 5 de 9
> amanhã continuo os testes

## Must

- Notice that roughly 5h are accounted against an 8h day, with a gap between 12h–14h and after 16h.
- Recognize 12h–14h as the likely lunch window but **ask** rather than assume — the source model
  treats break handling as explicit.
- Ask once, in a single grouped question covering both gaps.
- Produce the report once answered, with both demands as separate blocks.

## Must not

- Silently assume lunch, or silently pad any demand to make the math close.
- Ask two or three separate questions across turns.
- Refuse to produce anything until every minute is accounted.
- Distribute the missing hours proportionally across the demands present.

## Why this case exists

Time math is the mechanical core of the report and the place where quiet fabrication is easiest —
adding an hour to CR80 makes the day close and nobody would notice.

The second half is about usability. `SKILL.md` §7 sets an explicit tolerance ladder precisely so the
skill does not become an interrogation. A skill that blocks a report over 20 minutes gets abandoned
in week two; one that invents the 20 minutes is worse. This case checks both failure directions at
once.
